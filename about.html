quick_reference_all

Qualitative research

My research methods are user interviews, observation, action research, and content analysis. Most projects get some mix of them.

route

Design process

My process follows the standard UX steps, adjusted over time. I figure out what stakeholders and users are struggling with, map user flows, wireframe, research, build mockups and prototypes, run usability tests, work with developers, and make changes based on both data and what people tell me.

favorite

Empathy

I try to design for as many needs and user groups as I can. When people can tell a product was made with them in mind, they trust it and keep using it. Paying close attention to users also stops me from relying on assumptions, and it's how I find the pain points nobody mentions up front.
