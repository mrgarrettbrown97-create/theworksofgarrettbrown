Dishmatch: finding somewhere to sit down and eat

Overview

Dishmatch is a mobile app for finding food nearby. Some people want something quick and specific. Others want a long list to browse and narrow down. It handles both. I also used the project to try a new way of working: designing with Claude Code connected to Figma through the Figma MCP server.

Goals:

  1. Help people find places to sit down and eat, as well as delivery.
  2. Let users filter by cuisine, format, and occasion in one view.
  3. Test whether going back and forth between AI and Figma speeds up hi-fi prototyping.
ROLESole designer
TIMELINEMar 23 – Apr 1, 2026

The challenge

Most people search for food through a browser or a delivery app like DoorDash or Uber Eats. You can search for specific foods or cuisines, but the filters are built for ordering. They don't help much if you want a restaurant to actually go to.

PROBLEM TO SOLVE

It's hard to find places to eat beyond takeout. Current apps don't let you filter for the kind of meal you want.

Gathering insights

I looked at Google, DoorDash, and Uber Eats to see where they fall short for people who want more than takeout. Then I interviewed 10 people and turned the most common problems into personas and user journeys.

Going back and forth between AI and Figma

I started by sketching problems and solutions on paper, which settled the core features, filters, and workflow. I turned those into structured prompts for Claude Code and added a custom rule so Claude could check reference images in a folder while refining its output.

The prototype

Robust filter suite

Set results to match what you're in the mood for. Want an Indian or Korean food truck nearby for lunch and a nice sit-down Japanese place for dinner? Dishmatch shows both on one screen.

View and save

Each restaurant page puts the ordering details first. Save a place to come back to later, and remove it when you're done.

Where it stands

Early feedback has been good. Next up is usability testing to see whether Dishmatch actually makes it easier to find somewhere to eat. I now use the same Figma MCP and Claude Code workflow for prototypes and redesigns as Staff UI/UX Designer at Democracy First.
